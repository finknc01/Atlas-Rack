# Mission 00 — The Requirements Packet

## Briefing
Sales promised dense GPU capacity. Facilities says “one rack.” Nobody has written down what success means.

## Objective
Translate vague demand into explicit technical requirements and constraints.

## Tasks
Define a fictional workload and target GPU count. Record assumptions for rack U, power envelope, cooling method, network ports, management connectivity, storage, redundancy, service access, weight, growth, and budget.

Create three categories: hard constraint, design preference, unknown requiring validation.

## Twist
Halfway through, remove one assumption you were treating as fact and mark it unknown.

## Evidence
- requirements table
- assumptions/unknowns log
- first block diagram
- acceptance criteria

## Victory condition
Another engineer can tell what the rack is supposed to accomplish before seeing your solution.
