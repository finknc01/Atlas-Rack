# Mission 01 — Rack It Once

## Briefing
The hardware fits on a spreadsheet. Now prove it fits physically and can still be serviced.

## Objective
Learn rack units, component placement, airflow orientation, PDU/switch space, cable space, weight distribution, and maintenance access.

## Build
Create a 42U/48U-style elevation for compute, network, management, storage if used, and power distribution. Leave realistic service/airflow considerations rather than packing every U by instinct.

## Design challenge
Remove one compute chassis in your drawing. Can a technician physically reach and replace it without dismantling unrelated infrastructure?

## Evidence
- front/rear rack elevation
- U allocation table
- weight estimate with assumptions
- serviceability notes

## Victory condition
The rack is a maintainable physical system, not just a sum of component heights.
