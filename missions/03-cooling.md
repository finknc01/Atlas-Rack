# Mission 03 — Heat Has to Go Somewhere

## Briefing
Power approved the revision. Thermal engineering asks where all those kilowatts become heat.

## Objective
Connect electrical load to heat rejection, airflow/liquid cooling, hot/cold aisle concepts, and liquid-loop components such as CDU/manifold at a design level.

## Tasks
Estimate rack heat load from your power model. Compare an air-cooled option with a liquid-assisted/direct-liquid-cooling concept. Trace heat from GPU → cold plate/heatsink → air/liquid → facility rejection path.

## Constraint change
Assume the room can remove less heat by air than your rack produces. Redesign rather than simply writing “use liquid cooling.” Specify what changes in the rack and facility interface.

## Evidence
- heat-flow diagram
- cooling option comparison
- revised rack drawing
- failure considerations such as fan/pump/CDU/manifold dependency

## Victory condition
You can explain the complete heat path instead of treating cooling as an invisible facility service.
