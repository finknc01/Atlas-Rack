# Mission 04 — The Cable Jungle

## Briefing
The rack passes power and thermal review. The implementation team asks where hundreds of power, management, storage, and data links physically go.

## Objective
Plan cable paths, switch port counts, media types, labeling, bend/service space, and separation of logical fabrics.

## Tasks
Create a cable/port schedule for each server. Distinguish management, production/service, storage if used, and high-performance compute fabric. Choose plausible DAC/AOC/fiber/copper media assumptions and justify them by distance/speed/serviceability.

## Constraint change
A rear service path becomes blocked by your cable bundles. Revise switch/PDU placement or routing to restore serviceability.

## Evidence
- port map
- cable count by type
- rear-rack path sketch
- labeling convention

## Victory condition
A technician could cable the modeled rack from your documentation without inventing the topology on the fly.
