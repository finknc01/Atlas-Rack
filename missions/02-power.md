# Mission 02 — The Breaker Says No

## Briefing
Your first rack is beautiful. Facilities rejects it because peak load exceeds the available electrical envelope.

## Objective
Reason from server power to rack PDU and feed constraints, including redundancy and three-phase concepts at an architecture level.

## Tasks
Estimate idle/typical/peak power for each device from a clearly documented fictional BOM. Add rack totals and redundancy assumptions. Translate the design into feed/PDU requirements and headroom.

## Constraint change
Reduce the permitted rack power by 20% after the first design. You may reduce density, choose different hardware assumptions, spread load, or revise performance expectations—but document the tradeoff.

## Evidence
- per-device and rack power table
- A/B feed concept
- headroom calculation
- design revision and lost/gained capacity

## Victory condition
You can explain why nameplate, expected load, redundancy, and usable capacity are different numbers.
