# Mission 06 — Procurement Strikes Back

## Briefing
Your architecture is technically sound. Procurement removes one preferred component and cuts the budget.

## Objective
Practice substitution, BOM reasoning, lead-time risk, and preserving design intent under commercial constraints.

## Scenario
Choose one key component class—GPU server, switch, PDU, cooling interface, or optics—and make the preferred option unavailable. Reduce total fictional budget by 10–20%.

## Tasks
Produce at least two alternatives. Recalculate power, cooling, ports, rack space, performance assumptions, and risks. Identify what requirement you would negotiate if no substitute preserves all goals.

## Evidence
- BOM v1/v2
- substitution decision matrix
- cost delta
- downstream design changes

## Victory condition
You can show that component substitution is a systems-engineering change, not just a procurement line-item change.
